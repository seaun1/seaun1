## 🚀 My Engineering Story
My journey into software engineering didn't start in a traditional university classroom—it started on the frontlines of high-intensity global operations. 

Tasked with managing high-concurrency transaction processing and cross-timezone customer service (covering AU, BR, and Asian markets), my daily shifts stretched from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. As the sole financial provider for my family and a father raising a 2-year-old son, I hit a physical limit. I didn't have more hours in the day, so I needed a way to scale myself.

That’s when I turned to code. I taught myself Python, DOM reverse-engineering, and asynchronous programming in my minimal spare time. I systematically dismantled my grueling 12-hour daily workflow, building headless bots, zero-trace network interceptors, and distributed cross-tab state machines. 

**The result? I reduced my manual processing time by over 80%**, allowing me to reclaim my time for my family and further system architecture studies. I don't just write scripts; I analyze complex business logic, identify system vulnerabilities, and engineer stealthy, fault-tolerant solutions under extreme pressure.
